The Solanki Foundation
You Are Not Alone

If you are navigating relationship trauma, domestic violence, emotional abuse, or the weight of unspoken mental health struggles — there are people who understand your world and resources built for you. Everything here is confidential.

What We Offer

Support That Understands Your World

The Solanki Foundation connects members of the South Asian community to culturally competent resources — mental health support, community, and pathways to healing. You do not have to explain your culture, your family dynamics, or your reasons. We understand.

Community Support

Connect with peer support circles and community groups where you can share openly — without judgment, without stigma, and without having to explain your culture or your world.

Mental Health Access

Subsidized therapy and connections to culturally competent mental health professionals who understand South Asian family dynamics and relationship trauma.

Safety & Stability

Connections to crisis resources, legal aid referrals, and transitional support through our partner organizations — helping you rebuild on your own terms.

"Strength is not silence. It is the courage to ask for what you deserve."
How It Works

Three Simple Steps

There is no formal application. No intake process. Just reach out — and we will help connect you to the right resources.

01

Reach Out

Send us a message using the form below, or email foundation@solanki.co. Share as much or as little as you are comfortable with. Your name is optional.

02

Connect

A member of our team will respond within 48 hours to understand your needs and share relevant options — peer groups, therapy access, or crisis resources.

03

Access Support

We connect you directly with our partner organizations and resources. The Foundation covers or subsidizes costs where possible. Everything remains confidential.
