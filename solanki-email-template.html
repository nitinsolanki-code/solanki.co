TONE GUIDELINES

Voice Warm, direct, and confident. No corporate jargon. Write like you're speaking to someone you respect.
Length Short. 3-5 sentences in the body. One clear ask. If it needs to be longer, break into paragraphs with breathing room.
Subject Lowercase, conversational. No caps, no brackets, no emojis. "quick question" or "following up on [topic]"
Sign-off "Best," or "Warmly," — then first name only. Never "Thanks!" or "Cheers!"
Avoid Exclamation marks (one max per email), emojis, "Hope this finds you well," "Just circling back," "Per my last email," bolding inside the body, colored text.
