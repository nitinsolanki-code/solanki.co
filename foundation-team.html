The Solanki Foundation

THE PEOPLE

BEHIND THE MISSION

Built on family values, guided by expertise, and committed to the long view. The people behind the Solanki Foundation bring lived experience, professional discipline, and a shared belief that silence is not strength.

FOUNDER

Nitin Solanki

Founder & Chairman

Nitin Solanki is the founder of Solanki Co — a family holding company built on the belief that discipline, cultural understanding, and sustained commitment create meaningful change. The Solanki Foundation is the impact arm of that vision.

Born from personal experience and a deep understanding of the cultural silence around mental health in the Indian community, the Foundation reflects Nitin's commitment to building something that outlasts him — a legacy of accountability, dignity, and support for those who need it most.

BOARD OF ADVISORS

Guided by Expertise

The Foundation's Board of Advisors brings together professionals with deep expertise in the areas that matter most to our mission. As we formalize our advisory board, we are seeking leaders in these critical disciplines.

Mental Health Professional

A licensed clinician with expertise in culturally competent care and Indian community mental health needs.

Legal Counsel

An attorney experienced in nonprofit governance, domestic violence law, and immigrant rights.

Financial Advisor

A CPA or financial professional to ensure fiscal discipline, transparent reporting, and sustainable growth.

Community Leader

A respected voice within the Indian community with deep ties to grassroots organizations and cultural institutions.

Corporate Partner

A business leader experienced in CSR, corporate giving programs, and strategic partnerships.

Survivor Advocate

Someone with lived experience who can ensure the Foundation's work remains grounded in the reality of those it serves.
