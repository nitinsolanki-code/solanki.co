Partner With Us

Partnerships, not parallel infrastructure. We fund, connect, and amplify established organizations already embedded in the communities they serve.

Organizations That Know

Who We Partner With

We work with established organizations that have demonstrated impact, cultural competency, and deep trust within their communities. If you serve the South Asian community, there's likely a partnership opportunity here.

Mental Health Providers

Therapists, counselors, and clinics offering culturally competent mental health services to the South Asian community.

Legal Aid Organizations

Organizations providing legal services and advocacy for survivors of domestic violence, family law matters, and related challenges.

Community Organizations

South Asian community groups, cultural centers, wellness organizations, and grassroots networks serving their communities.

Crisis & Shelter Networks

Emergency shelters, crisis hotlines, and 24/7 support services for individuals in immediate need of safety and care.

"Our role is to fund, connect, and amplify — not to build from scratch."
How We Work Together

What Partnership Looks Like

Funding

Direct grants for programs and services that serve the South Asian community. Resources that help you do what you already do, better.

Referral Network

We connect people directly to your services. You are part of a trusted network of organizations our community can turn to.

Amplification

Increased visibility and reach. We help more people know about your work and the impact you're creating.

Resource Sharing

Training, tools, and collaborative support. We work together to strengthen the entire ecosystem.

Building Our First Partnerships

Where We Stand

The Solanki Foundation is currently in formation and actively seeking our inaugural partner organizations. We are looking for established organizations with demonstrated impact, cultural competency, and a commitment to serving the South Asian community.

We believe in transparency. This is a new initiative, and we're building it thoughtfully and intentionally. Your feedback and input will shape how we work. We're not here to replace or duplicate existing efforts—we're here to strengthen them.
