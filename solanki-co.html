MORA SOL

A Name Rooted in Family

Mora Sol is derived from Morarbhai Solanki — a grandfather whose quiet strength, conviction, and generosity shaped a family's values. In Spanish, the words translate to "dwelling sun" — warmth, resilience, and light. Every Mora Sol entity carries that same spirit: build something real, treat people with integrity, and create value that outlasts you.
