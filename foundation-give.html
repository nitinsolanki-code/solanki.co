SUPPORT

THE MISSION

Every dollar is deployed with discipline — clear outcomes, transparent reporting, and a long-term view. Your support helps us fund, connect, and amplify the organizations already doing the work.

WHERE IT GOES

Directed Impact, Not Overhead

The Solanki Foundation operates with minimal overhead. Your contribution goes directly toward funding partner organizations, subsidizing therapy access, supporting crisis resources, and expanding the reach of culturally competent mental health services across the South Asian community.

Partner Funding

Direct grants to established organizations serving South Asian men and women through mental health support, legal aid, and community resources.

Access & Subsidies

Covering therapy costs, counseling navigation, and removing financial barriers to culturally competent mental health care.

Community Programs

Peer support circles, awareness initiatives, and grassroots programs that create safe spaces for healing and connection.

"Every contribution is a commitment — to the people who need support, and to the culture we want to build."
