QUESTIONS

What Leaders Usually Ask

Do you implement, or just advise?

We sit above implementation and make it land. We scope, select, and govern implementers and oversee delivery — but we don't sell build hours or software. In the strategy phase we are vendor-neutral; if AI agents are the right answer, we can help design, select, or implement them.

How is this different from a chatbot rollout?

Individual tools help people do tasks faster. We change how work flows, how decisions get made, and where value is created — the operating-model questions that determine whether AI actually pays off.

How fast is value?

The sprint produces a funded plan and a build-ready first pilot in two to three weeks. The entry diagnostic gives you a readiness read and ranked opportunities in about a week.

Is this safe for a regulated environment?

Yes — governance and compliance are built in from the start. We focus on administrative and operational leverage with clear decision boundaries, and structure everything around your regulatory requirements.

How do you structure engagements?

Every engagement is scoped to the specific challenge — we price the outcome, not hours. Most relationships begin with a short, fixed-scope diagnostic that turns ambiguity into a plan you own. We will be direct about scope and investment once we understand the work.
