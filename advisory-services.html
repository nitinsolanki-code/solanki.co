ADVISORY SERVICES

Four engagement models. One operating philosophy. We do not sell hours — we solve problems.

ENGAGEMENT MODELS

Format Follows Need

Every advisory engagement is designed around the specific challenge. The format — whether ongoing counsel, a focused diagnostic, defined-scope transformation work, or embedded leadership — follows the need, not the other way around. We work with a small number of clients at any given time, because depth matters more than breadth.

"The right counsel at the right inflection point changes the trajectory of a company."
ADVISORY RETAINER

Ongoing Strategic Partnership

Strategic Sounding Board

Regular check-ins with the founder or CEO — designed as a thinking partner, not a reporting relationship. We help you pressure-test decisions, evaluate opportunities, and navigate ambiguity with pattern recognition from decades of operating experience.

Board-Level Involvement

For companies that need strategic counsel at the governance level — board observation, investor relations support, and strategic planning sessions that bring outside perspective without adding bureaucracy.

Decision Support

Critical inflection points — fundraising, M&A, expansion, restructuring — where the stakes are high and the playbook is unclear. We bring clarity through structured analysis, scenario planning, and operator-grade judgment.

DIAGNOSTIC SPRINT

The Clearest Way to Begin

Value & Gap Assessment

A short, fixed-scope engagement that surfaces where value is leaking, what is working, and what to move on first. You leave with a prioritized roadmap and a clear business case — low commitment, fast clarity.

Growth Diagnostics

For companies that have stalled or plateaued — a diagnostic of growth levers, unit economics, and go-to-market effectiveness. Clear report, actionable recommendations, and a plan you own whether or not the relationship continues.

AI Value Path Sprint

A senior-led sprint for leadership teams turning scattered AI experiments into a prioritized roadmap, business case, and first 90-day plan. Strategy-first, vendor-neutral, execution-aware. Learn more →

TRANSFORMATION COUNSEL

Defined-Scope Work on the Problems That Set Trajectory

Operating-Model Redesign

Process optimization, team design, and operational rhythm for companies that have outgrown their current structure. We diagnose the bottleneck and build the playbook to fix it.

Post-Acquisition Integration

Integrating teams, systems, and cultures after a deal closes. We bring structure to the first 100 days and keep the value thesis intact through execution.

Go-to-Market Strategy

Market entry, positioning, channel strategy, and launch planning for companies entering new markets or segments. Fixed-scope, defined deliverables, fast execution.

FRACTIONAL LEADERSHIP

Senior Counsel Through a Transition

Embedded leadership for organizations that need experienced guidance without a full-time hire. Board-level involvement on strategy, vendor and build-versus-buy decisions, and organizational alignment — an advisory relationship, not a staffing arrangement. Monthly commitment, quarterly reviews.
