MORA SOL

A Name Rooted in Family

Mora Sol is derived from my grandfather, Morarbhai Solanki — a man who left Gujarat for Zambia with very little, searching for opportunity and a better life for his family. He was a pioneer in the truest sense: no blueprint, no safety net, just conviction and the willingness to sacrifice everything for the next generation.

In Spanish, the words translate to "dwelling sun" — warmth, resilience, and light. Every Mora Sol entity carries his spirit: move forward with quiet strength, treat people with integrity, and build something that outlasts you.
