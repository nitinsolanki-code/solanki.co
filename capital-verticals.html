INVESTMENT VERTICALS

Three focused asset classes. One investment discipline. Each vertical has its own thesis, risk framework, and partnership model.

THE VERTICALS

Deep Expertise, Narrow Focus

We do not chase yield across every asset class. Mora Sol Capital deploys across three focused verticals where we have developed conviction through experience, relationships, and repetition. Each vertical has its own thesis, but they share a common discipline: protect the downside, partner with operators, and structure for resilience.

"We invest in what we understand, alongside people who understand it better."
HOSPITALITY

Hotels, Boutique Assets, and Operator Partnerships

Hotel Fund Partnerships

We deploy capital alongside experienced hotel fund managers who specialize in select-service, boutique, and branded hospitality assets. Our role is capital and structure — theirs is sourcing, operations, and execution.

Operator Co-Investments

Direct co-investment alongside proven hotel operators on specific assets — acquisitions, conversions, and repositioning plays where the operator brings domain expertise and an established track record.

Select Hospitality Assets

Opportunistic investment in hospitality-adjacent real estate — extended-stay concepts, resort properties, and branded residences in high-demand markets with strong fundamentals.

STORAGE & FLEX SPACE

Self-Storage, Development, and Logistics

Self-Storage Acquisitions

Partnering with operators to acquire and optimize existing self-storage facilities in growing MSAs. Cash-flowing assets with proven demand fundamentals and operational upside.

Ground-Up Development

Selective ground-up self-storage development in underserved markets alongside experienced developers. Higher risk, higher return — but always with a proven development partner.

Flex Industrial

Logistics-adjacent flex space in markets driven by e-commerce, last-mile delivery, and small business demand. Stable cash flows with growing tailwinds.

STRUCTURED FUNDING

Bridge Capital, Structured Lending, and Servicing

Bridge Capital

Short-term bridge loans for commercial real estate transactions — acquisitions, refinancing, and value-add projects that need fast, flexible capital with clear exit paths.

Structured Lending

Specialty finance positions structured around specific deal economics. Revenue through origination fees, interest spread, and structured participation.

Servicing & Origination

Building a repeatable origination and servicing platform alongside experienced partners. Each transaction deepens the playbook and strengthens the infrastructure.
