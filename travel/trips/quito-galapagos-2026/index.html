Friday, June 19
Philadelphia → Quito
▾
✈ Travel Day

Private transfer to PHL. Direct American Airlines flight departing 4:35pm, arriving Quito 11:58pm. Private car and welcome host waiting at the airport for a seamless midnight transfer to hotel.

Same time zone as US Central. No jet lag. The trip starts when the group is ready.
2
Saturday, June 20
Quito — Old Town & Chef's Table
▾
🏛 Culture

Recovery day by design. Late breakfast at the hotel.

Afternoon: private guided walk through Quito's Old Town (UNESCO World Heritage) with a local historian — not a tour guide. Someone who can bring four centuries of colonial history to life while navigating hidden courtyards most visitors walk past.

Evening: curated dinner reservation at a chef's table. The kind of meal where you remember what you ate three years later.

3
Sunday, June 21
Quito → Baños — Villa & Waterfalls
▾
🏔 Adventure

Private vehicles to Baños. Vetted villa secured for 11 guests — 4 bedrooms, 4.5 bathrooms, 7 beds.

Afternoon: waterfall route with a local guide who knows the unmarked trails. Welcome dinner at the villa, private chef.

3-hour scenic drive through the highlands.
4
Monday, June 22
Amazon Day Excursion
▾
🌿 Immersion

Full-day Amazon excursion through a trusted indigenous-led operator. Cultural immersion with local tribes, guided rainforest hike, 30-minute kayak through canopy reflections.

Packed lunch from the villa chef. Evening: fire-pit dinner under the stars at the villa.

1 hour from Baños. Our local DMC handles all transport.
5
Tuesday, June 23
Baños → Cotopaxi → Quito
▾
🏔 Adventure

Check out. Cotopaxi stop en route — private half-day tour. The group chooses: 2-hour hike or mountain biking.

Solanki-arranged picnic lunch at altitude with views of the volcano. Arrive Quito late afternoon. Check into JW Marriott Downtown.

Cotopaxi is directly on the route back — no detour.
6
Wednesday, June 24
Mindo Cloud Forest
▾
🌿 Nature

Morning: Middle of the World monument — quick stop, worth it.

Then: Mindo Cloud Forest. Private hummingbird sanctuary tour and a chocolate-making experience with a local farmer who walks you through the full bean-to-bar process.

Return to JW Marriott for the evening.

2 hours to Mindo. All transport arranged.
7
Thursday, June 25
Quito → Galápagos
▾
✈ Travel Day

Private transfer to airport. Flight to Galápagos. Met at arrival by your dedicated naturalist guide — who stays with the group for the full six days.

Settled into curated Galápagos accommodation. First evening: orientation dinner with your guide.

Galápagos is 1 hour behind Central time.
8–13
Friday Jun 26 — Wednesday Jul 1
The Galápagos Islands
▾
🐢 Wildlife · 🤿 Snorkeling · 🌋 Exploration

Six days, fully curated. This is the anchor of the trip — and we treat it that way.

Private yacht day-trips to key islands. Snorkeling with sea lions, giant tortoise highlands, lava tunnels, blue-footed booby colonies. Each day planned but flexible — your naturalist guide reads the group and adjusts.

Dining at the best local spots, all pre-arranged. No decisions needed.

Your guide is with you every day. Expert, flexible, attentive. Not a shared boat tour — your group, your pace.

Our operating partners handle all island permits, boat logistics, and daily scheduling. You just show up.
14
Thursday, July 2
Galápagos → Quito — Farewell Dinner
▾

Morning departure from Galápagos. Flight back to Quito.

Final evening: farewell dinner at a rooftop restaurant overlooking the city. Private transfer back to JW Marriott.

15–16
Friday, July 3
Quito → Philadelphia
▾
✈ Travel Day

Early morning. Private transfer to airport (departure by 2–3am). American Airlines direct flight 6am–4:45pm.

Trip close-out message and curated photo collection shared post-trip.

9 hours 45 minutes home. The trip ends when the photos arrive.
