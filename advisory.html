OUR POSITION

Earned Through Experience

Mora Sol Advisory exists because the best counsel comes from people who have done the work — who have sat in the operating seat, navigated the ambiguity, and built through cycles. This is not advisory as a service. It is advisory as a relationship, reserved for the right leaders at the right moments.

Born from a career in consulting — across strategy, operations, and transformation — the Advisory practice draws on deep pattern recognition, cross-industry exposure, and a bias for execution over theory.

"The right counsel at the right inflection point changes the trajectory of a company."
ENGAGEMENT MODELS

Two Ways to Work Together

Every engagement is designed around the specific challenge. We do not sell hours. We solve problems — and the format follows the need.

Advisory Retainer

Ongoing strategic counsel for founders, CEOs, and leadership teams. Board-style involvement — regular check-ins, strategic sounding board, decision support at key inflection points. Retainer-based with quarterly commitments.

Consulting Sprints

Defined-scope projects with clear deliverables — go-to-market strategy, operational restructuring, growth diagnostics, or M&A readiness. Fixed-fee engagements designed to move fast and deliver clarity.

WHO WE SERVE

Selective by Design

We work with founders who have built something real and are navigating what comes next — whether that is scaling, pivoting, raising, or preparing for an exit. We work with PE-backed operators who need a strategic partner outside the boardroom. And we work with leadership teams at inflection points where the old playbook no longer applies.

Selectivity is not a marketing posture. It is how we protect the quality of the work and the depth of the relationship. If the fit is not right, we will say so — and help connect you with someone who is.
