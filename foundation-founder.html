A PERSONAL NOTE

Why This Work Exists

I grew up in an Indian household where certain things were never discussed. Mental health. Marital struggles. The quiet suffering that happened behind closed doors. Men did not talk about their feelings. Women did not talk about their pain. These were things you simply did not name.

I watched this silence shape families — mine included. I saw the cost of it. The isolation. The damage passed from one generation to the next because no one had the language, the resources, or the permission to break the cycle.

The Solanki Foundation was born from the belief that silence is not strength — and that our community deserves better than endurance. Women navigating trauma deserve support that understands their world. Men carrying the weight of unspoken struggles deserve a path to healing that does not require them to break first.

This is not charity. This is accountability. To the people who raised us, to the ones raising the next generation, and to the culture we want to build going forward.

NITIN SOLANKI Founder, Solanki Co

"Silence is not strength. It is the courage to name what needs to change — and then do the work."

PHILOSOPHY

How I Think About This Work

I approach the Foundation the same way I approach everything at Solanki Co — with discipline, patience, and a long-term view. We are not here to build something fast. We are here to build something that lasts.

That means partnering with organizations that already have deep roots in the communities they serve. It means deploying resources with precision, not just generosity. It means being transparent about what we do, how we do it, and what the impact looks like.

Every dollar, every partnership, every initiative is held to a simple standard: is this creating meaningful, measurable change for the people who need it most?

LOOKING AHEAD

A Foundation Built to Endure

The Solanki Foundation is designed to outlast its founder. My daughters — Jia and Dhiya — represent the next generation of this work. The values, the commitment, and the cultural understanding at the heart of this Foundation will be carried forward by them.

This is not a project. This is a family legacy — one that I hope creates the kind of change that compounds over decades, not just campaigns.

About Solanki Co

The Solanki Foundation is the impact arm of Solanki Co — a family holding company that operates across capital, advisory, digital, studios, and travel. Each division reflects the same core values: discipline, cultural understanding, and a commitment to building things that endure.

The Foundation is funded by Solanki Co and its founder, ensuring operational independence and alignment with our long-term mission.
